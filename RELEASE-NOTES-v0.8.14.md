# QIF v0.8.14

QIF v0.8.14 introduces the Agent Skill Behavioral Portability Pilot.

- Adds the `agent-skill-portability` schema, example, local verifier, CLI
  routing, starter generation, and retained negative fixtures.
- Compares frozen baseline and Skill-enabled runs across at least two declared
  independent client groups.
- Reproduces criterion deltas, regressions, blocking failures, evidence state,
  and governance outcomes.
- Keeps example-only results provisional and refuses semantic truth,
  causality, competence, independence, and authority claims.

Verification: `npm test`.
