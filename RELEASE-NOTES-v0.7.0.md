# QIF v0.7.0

## Empirical Calibration Pilot Contract

QIF now provides an executable, domain-general contract for beginning an
empirical calibration pilot without inventing operational evidence.

This release adds:

- prospectively registered pilot protocols and domain plans;
- explicit software and non-software domain coverage;
- decision enrollments with frozen forecasts, outcome definitions, and timing;
- separate planned, observing, completed, missing, and excluded outcome states;
- fail-closed empirical-origin and evidence requirements;
- reproducible pilot readiness assessments and governance routing;
- 20 retained negative fixtures for the new verifier rules; and
- an updated portable AI package for Gemini, Claude, Codex, and other
  document-context AI systems.

The committed example is intentionally `not-started` and `insufficient`.
Verifier success does not prove study validity, representativeness, causality,
calibration truth, quality, authorization, or authority.
