# QIF v0.8.3: Portable Knowledge Supply-Chain Revocation

QIF can now respond when reusable quality knowledge is disputed, compromised,
superseded, stale, withdrawn, or invalidated after import.

## Added

- Revocation policies and exact-subject notices for libraries, items, source
  digests, evidence origins, and publishers.
- Evidence-backed dependency edges linking portable items to local
  applicability, reproduction, and eligibility records.
- Deterministic direct and transitive impact assessment.
- Fail-closed suspension for active or disputed notices.
- Governed revalidation with replacement evidence and preserved history.
- 13 retained negative fixtures and portable-package guidance.

## Boundary

The verifier proves structural traceability, digest matching, graph closure,
set and count reproduction, suspension, governance routing, and history
preservation. It does not prove notice authenticity, compromise truth, source
safety, semantic fitness, authorization, or authority.

The committed example deliberately uses an unverified notice and remains
governance-required.
