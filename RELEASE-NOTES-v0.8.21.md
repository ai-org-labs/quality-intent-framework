# QIF v0.8.21

QIF v0.8.21 adds Challenge Corpus Mutation and Coverage Calibration.

## Highlights

- Traceable seed, mutant, sequestered holdout, and benign-control corpus items.
- Reproducible threat detection, miss, false-alarm, and correct-benign partitions.
- Challenger, target, and judge role-separation checks.
- Deterministic holdout detection, benign false-alarm, mutation coverage,
  grade, and governance-trigger reproduction.
- 163 retained Skill supply-chain negative fixtures.

## Boundary

Passing local verification proves structural consistency, not corpus
completeness, mutation adequacy, holdout representativeness, generalization,
false-alarm acceptability, adversarial robustness, or operational safety.
