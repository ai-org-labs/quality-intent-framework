# QIF v0.8.18

QIF v0.8.18 carries a Skill dependency quarantine decision through an
evidence-backed recovery and re-entry review chain.

## Added

- Observed quarantine outcomes linked to the original candidate and decision.
- Recorded remediation actions with evidence and explicit non-execution
  boundaries.
- Failure-trajectory replay validation with reproducible result matching.
- Bounded observation windows with reproducible duration and completion.
- Residual-risk assessments with reproduced highest severity.
- Governance-owned re-entry assessments with deterministic prerequisites,
  readiness, and fail-closed triggers.
- 30 retained negative fixtures for recovery lineage, arithmetic, readiness,
  governance routing, and authority boundaries.

## Boundary

Structural validation does not prove containment or remediation effectiveness,
replay representativeness, operational stability, future incident freedom, or
safe re-entry. QIF does not execute remediation or authorize restart,
permissions, trust, quarantine enforcement, or automatic re-entry.
