# QIF v0.6.47

QIF v0.6.47 makes deployment intervention and recovery evidence executable.

- Adds intervention and recovery policies with response SLAs, authority roles, fail-closed requirements, and non-authority boundaries.
- Adds intervention events with exact timing, authority evidence, affected scope, reversibility, rollback plans, and externalized evidence.
- Adds recovery verification with rollback evidence, reproducible check counts, restoration state, and residual risk.
- Adds aggregate assessments and governance for unverified evidence, late or unauthorized response, failed recovery, residual risk, and insufficient evidence.
- Explicitly rejects intervention counts as quality and prevents QIF from authorizing intervention or redeployment.

The committed package is illustrative. It demonstrates the runtime without claiming intervention effectiveness or operational safety.
