# QIF v0.6.46

QIF v0.6.46 makes the gap between short evaluation and longer operational use explicit.

- Adds reviewed transfer and drift policies.
- Adds evaluation, shadow-operation, limited-operation, and operation trajectories with ordered checkpoints.
- Recomputes time horizons, within-trajectory drift, exact matching dimensions, terminal behavior changes, transfer gaps, stage coverage, and evidence sufficiency.
- Routes unverified evidence, matching failures, transfer gaps, long-horizon drift, and insufficient assessments to governance.
- Keeps deployment, pause, rollback, recovery, causality, intent, semantic quality, selection, authorization, and authority outside verifier claims.

The committed example is illustrative and intentionally insufficient. It demonstrates the structure without claiming real deployment safety.
