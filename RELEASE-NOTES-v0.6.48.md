# QIF v0.6.48

QIF v0.6.48 makes distributed intervention coordination and blast-radius evidence executable.

- Adds distributed intervention policies with per-lane authority, dependency order, conflict, stand-down, concurrency, affected-scope, and shared-resource limits.
- Adds coordination runs that bind multiple intervention events to lane actions, evidence, conflicts, and resource claims.
- Adds blast-radius observations with intended, observed, and unexpected scope plus propagation and containment evidence.
- Adds reproducible assessments and governance for unresolved conflict, unverified evidence, shared-resource saturation, common-mode failure, scope exceedance, and insufficient evidence.
- Explicitly rejects lane count, intervention throughput, and containment status as quality or systemic-safety proof.

The committed package is illustrative. It demonstrates the runtime without claiming coordination success, containment effectiveness, or operational safety.
