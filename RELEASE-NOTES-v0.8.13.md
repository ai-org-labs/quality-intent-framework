# QIF v0.8.13

QIF v0.8.13 adds governed closure-policy learning after incident calibration.

- Derive bounded policy revision candidates from adverse closure calibrations.
- Replay candidates against frozen historical closure and late-signal inputs.
- Reproduce candidate decisions and outcome deltas without rewriting history.
- Route example-only or uncertain replay evidence to accountable governance.
- Record adopt, revise, reject, or governance-required decisions without live
  policy execution or automatic authority.
- Add retained negative fixtures, portable-package guidance, and verifier
  boundaries for causality, future safety, semantic truth, and adoption.

The verifier establishes structural and deterministic integrity only. It does
not prove that a counterfactual caused an outcome or that a policy is safe.
