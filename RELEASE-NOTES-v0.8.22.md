# QIF v0.8.22

QIF v0.8.22 adds Adaptive Challenge Promotion and Regression Governance.

## Highlights

- First-class challenge discoveries with source, pre-outcome selection,
  novelty, and evaluation-objective exposure.
- Accountable promote, retain, revise, retire, reject, and defer decisions with
  independent review and post-decision runs.
- Traceable regression families with active, retired, and protected-holdout
  membership.
- Reproducible lifecycle grades and governance for unresolved discoveries,
  exposed holdouts, objective leakage, missing review, failed regressions,
  missing unflagged audits, and unverified evidence.
- Retained negative fixtures for the new lifecycle rules.

## Boundary

Passing local verification proves structural consistency, not decision
correctness, promotion value, retirement safety, absence of overfitting,
objective concealment, unflagged-sample representativeness, regression
completeness, operational generalization, robustness, or safety.
