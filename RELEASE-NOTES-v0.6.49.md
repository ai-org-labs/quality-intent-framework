# QIF v0.6.49

QIF v0.6.49 makes intervention dependency diversity and correlated recovery risk executable.

- Adds policies for minimum recovery paths, verified independence, critical shared dependencies, observed failover, and fail-closed uncertainty.
- Adds recovery dependency graphs that reproduce path membership, overlap, critical shared dependencies, common causes, and evidence-backed independence claims.
- Adds recovery-path trials with prerequisites, activation authority evidence, observed switching, shared failure, and recovery results.
- Adds correlated recovery assessments and governance for unverified evidence, critical shared dependencies, unverified independence, unavailable fallback paths, and insufficient data.
- Explicitly rejects path count, vendor count, nominal diversity, and failover activity as quality, independence, recovery-effectiveness, or authority proof.

The committed package is illustrative. It demonstrates the runtime without claiming real recovery-path independence, causal common failure, recovery effectiveness, or operational safety.
